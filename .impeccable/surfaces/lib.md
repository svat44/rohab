# Surface: Rohab app screens (Operate)

Mode: Operate. Audience: older stroke survivors, one-handed, low vision, seated at home. Job: do today's exercises, see progress, stay safe. Constraint: Redmi 7A, portrait (camera geometry), 200% text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A patient-portal / clinic-paperwork app that looks like the NHS-style health services patients already use, refusing the soft, shadowed, rounded "wellness app" arrangement the app wore before. Pinned by the user: familiar medical look, not sleek.
OWN-WORLD: White page, near-black blue-ink text, one clinical blue (solid header, primary buttons, links), 2 px dark borders on every control and panel, 4 to 6 px corners, no shadows or textures. Buttons are solid blocks with a darker 4 px bottom edge that sinks when pressed. Yellow 4 px focus ring. Green only for "done", red only for STOP and faults. Atkinson Hyperlegible in two weights (400, 700). Lists are bordered rows with chevrons, not floating cards.
STORY: The patient opens the app and sees one thing to do today, a big blue button saying what it does, and the STOP/connection strip if a glove is connected. They believe it is safe and clinical, and press the button.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Solid blue title bar (screen name, Settings button labelled). Below on white: a bordered "Today" panel with heading, one plain sentence, and a full-width 64 dp primary button; then a labelled list of other tasks as bordered rows. Bottom: five-word labelled tab bar with the current tab filled. On width over 600 dp, tabs become a labelled left rail and content widens to a 760 dp column.
FORM: Patient-portal / NHS-style (user's chosen familiar look). seed: none (pinned) "avoid sleek, 2026-style interfaces ... a design patients would be familiar with" plus answer "Patient portal / NHS-style".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
